Men Mansion

+17.2% average monthly revenue growth, sustained for 14 months.

On the account from the store's first revenue month — built from zero paid channels.

Duration

Services

About the Brand

Men Mansion is a modern men's grooming brand built on old-school standards and new-school taste. Born from the fusion of time-tested, natural ingredients and a clean, confidence-first vision for today's man.

They use premium, carefully sourced raw materials to create high-performance skincare that blends tradition with innovation. Simple formulas, real results, no nonsense.

Overview

Men Mansion came to Seba Agency at the start, not the rescue. There were no paid channels running, no performance tracking worth the name, and no previous agency's account structure to inherit or unpick. The ad infrastructure was built from zero, alongside the brand.

That starting point cuts both ways, and it is worth stating plainly: a store's first months come off a small base, so early percentage gains are cheap. The figures below are drawn from a 14-month window precisely because a single quarter off a standing start proves very little.

The first build was custom server-side tracking, so that attribution held up before any spend depended on it. Meta and TikTok were then built out on top of it, channel by channel, with the creative system described below.

Our Process

Two parts of this system do most of the work. Both are spend-level independent — they behave identically at any budget, which is the reason this transfers to a larger account rather than being a small-account trick.

1

A naming convention that makes creative attributable

Every asset is named as concept number + format + angle + funnel stage, applied identically across every ad manager:

C221 UGC Woman Stress Acne DTC

The consequence is that performance becomes attributable by concept rather than by ad ID. You stop asking which ad won and start asking which angle won — a question whose answer survives the ad being retired, the platform changing, or the budget doubling.

2

A four-stage iteration ladder

A winning concept is not rebuilt from scratch. It is climbed, one variable at a time, so every result stays readable:

  1. 1

    Hook variants — same body, different opening seconds.

  2. 2

    Length and social proof — cut lengths, added review and result callouts.

  3. 3

    Different creators — the same proven angle, delivered by a new face.

  4. 4

    Full creative diversity — statics, VSL and AI-assisted formats off the same angle.

Changing one variable per stage is what keeps a losing test informative. A concept that dies at stage 3 tells you the angle was carried by the creator, not the message — which is a finding, not a write-off.

Alongside that

  • Avatar and sub-avatar mapping — so new concepts start from a named customer, not a blank page.

  • Weekly data-driven concepts — a standing queue their team could produce against, so the ladder never stalled for want of assets.

  • Klaviyo email flows — to hold the margin that paid acquisition bought.

  • AI workflow coaching — so their in-house team could produce more per week without more headcount.

  • Landing page support — so a working ad met a page built to match its promise.
