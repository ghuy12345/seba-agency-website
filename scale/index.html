    Questions

    How do you calculate net profit?

    Revenue minus COGS, shipping, fees, ad spend and other agreed costs. We agree the exact formula in writing before we start, and that's what our fee is based on.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on your stage and which channels you need. Every plan is paid on profit, so our fee grows only when yours does. You'll get exact numbers on the call.

    Do you make the ads too?

    We tell your team or creators exactly what to make every week, and on Scale you get 12 ready-to-shoot creative briefs a month. If you want us to produce the statics and video edits too, that's an add-on.

    Why a 3-month term?

    Month one is research and testing, months two and three are scaling what works. 90 days is also how long the growth guarantee runs.

    Do I keep my ad account and creatives?

    Always. We work inside your ad account, and everything we make is yours, even if you leave.

    What happens on the call?

    30 minutes. We look at your account and numbers together, tell you what we'd do first, and whether we're a fit. No hard sell.
