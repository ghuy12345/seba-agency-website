Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Seba Agency (contact: info@sebaagency.com). We provide digital marketing and design services to e-commerce brands.

2. What data we collect

When you visit this website, we may collect the following data:

  • Usage data via PostHog analytics (page views, clicks, scroll depth, session duration)
  • Device and browser information (browser type, operating system, screen resolution)
  • Approximate geographic location (country/region level, derived from IP address)
  • Referral source (how you arrived at our website)

We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it (e.g. by booking a call via Calendly).

3. How we use your data

We use collected data to:

  • Understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it
  • Measure the effectiveness of our marketing and content
  • Identify technical issues and improve site performance

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling purposes.

4. Analytics (PostHog)

We use PostHog (posthog.com) for product analytics. PostHog processes data on EU-based servers. PostHog collects anonymised usage data to help us understand how visitors interact with our website. You can read PostHog's privacy policy at posthog.com/privacy.

5. Third-party services

Our website links to and embeds the following third-party services:

  • Calendly — when you click a booking link, you are redirected to Calendly's platform. Calendly has its own privacy policy.
  • YouTube — our website embeds a YouTube video. YouTube may set cookies and collect data according to Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — we load the Fragment Mono typeface from Google Fonts. This results in your browser making a request to Google's servers.

6. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies. PostHog uses a first-party cookie to track sessions. No third-party advertising cookies are set by our website directly.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. You also have the right to object to processing and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@sebaagency.com.

8. Data retention

Analytics data is retained for 12 months and then deleted. We do not retain any personally identifiable information beyond what is necessary to respond to your enquiry.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at info@sebaagency.com.
